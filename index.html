🚛 Drivers Training

ELD Driver Onboarding Guide

Follow the nine sections in order while on the phone with the driver.

1Is the driver in his truck?

Start of every onboarding call

“Are you sitting in your truck right now?”
  • No: ask him to call back as soon as he gets into the truck.
  • Yes: continue below.

Ask the driver to send us, so we can add them to the system and get everything ready:

2Tablet & app download

Once the driver is ready

“What tablet do you use for your ELD – an iPad or an Android?”

🍎 iPad

Download our ELD app from the Apple App Store.

🤖 Android

  1. Download Telegram.
  2. We send the APK file in Telegram.
  3. Download the APK from the chat.
⚠️ On Android, Telegram may block the download. Choose More options, then press Install anyway.

3Permissions & hardware check

After the app is installed

4Log in & connect the ELD

Username, password and PT30 connection

  1. Driver types his username and password and taps Log in.
  2. If it says wrong username or password: reset it and give him a new username and password.
  3. Driver chooses his truck unit number.
  4. On the connection screen, he types the MAC address of the PT30 he checked earlier.
  5. Press Connect.
🔠 The MAC address must be typed in CAPITAL LETTERS.
✅ Done – his ELD is now connected.

5Has he used this ELD app before?

Quick experience check

“Have you used this kind of ELD app before?”

Yes: ask him to show you how to:

  • Fill in shipping details
  • Put his signature
  • Send logs to a police officer
If he does everything correctly, there is no need to explain it all. Skip to section 8.

No / not sure: go to section 6 and have him follow your steps.

6Step by step: filling & certifying logs

For drivers who don’t know the app

  1. Tap the 3 lines in the corner of the screen, then go to Logs.
  2. Choose today’s date.
  3. Tap Form and fill in the shipping details.
  4. Press Save.
  5. Go back to Logs and choose yesterday’s date.
  6. Go to Form and just press Save – no need to fill in yesterday’s form.
  7. Go to Certify and put his signature.
  8. Press Agree.
  9. Repeat the same steps for every other day in the daily logs list.

7DOT inspection: sending logs to the officer

When the driver is pulled over

  1. Set status to On Duty – not any other status.
  2. Tap the 3 lines to open the menu.
  3. Tap DOT Inspection (the 4th item in the list).
  4. Press Send Logs (the 2nd option).
  5. Type the comment code or email given by the officer.

8Pulled over or entering a weigh station

Always tell the ELD team

📞 As soon as the driver is pulled over, or about to enter a weigh station, he must inform the ELD team immediately.

9Basic info

Rules every driver should remember

  • 🖨️ Print the ELD instructions. An officer may ask to see them – without them the driver may get an HOS violation.
  • 📄 Keep paper logs for at least 8 days.
  • 🚚 When loaded, always tell us and send the BOL and the trailer number.
  • 💬 Connection issues or questions: text the group or call us.

🔄 How to refresh logs

  1. Way 1: close the app and open it again.
  2. Way 2: open the menu (3 lines), go to Account, tap the Refresh button on the right corner, then press OK.
